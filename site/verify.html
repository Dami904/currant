Receipt check

Check a receipt in your browser.

A receipt records the SHA-256 of the exact text a model was shown. Paste the receipt and the context; this page hashes the context here, with nothing sent anywhere, and compares. It starts with a receipt built from real documentation: Reppo's 100-credit and 440-credit pages, where neither says which is current, so Currant marks the conflict CONTESTED and cuts nothing. The same check runs in CI with currant replay.
