Lineage

What replaced what, with the quotes to prove it.

Every relation Currant verified on the live OpenServ, Reppo and Stripe docs. A model proposed each one; our code kept it only after finding every quote word for word in the page, and every quote was checked again when this page was built. Then a person read each one: the verdict is shown beside it, false positives included.

Replacements

Old value, new value, and why.

Oldest first. Between two values is the reason the detector ordered them, quoted from the pages, and what a person concluded on reading it. A value replaced by two different values is a branch: Currant reports it and does not pick a winner.

Contested

Two sources, two values, read by hand.

Both sides say they are current and nothing in either page shows which is newer, so Currant cuts neither: it shows the model both quotes and tells it not to guess. Each OpenServ and Reppo pair is a finding from the published drift pages, which also say whether the maintainers were told; findings not published there are not shown here.

Not shown

Where detection was wrong.

On the Kite docs, Currant verified 33 contradictions. Read by eye, 32 are dated changelog entries it could not order, and one compares testnet with mainnet. They are false CONTESTED notices, so they are not presented here as conflicts. Nothing was cut. The details are in the limitations.
