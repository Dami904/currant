# Create an account

> Sign up, sign in, and choose your SERV Reasoning organization.

1. Open the [SERV console](https://console.openserv.ai) and select **Sign up**.
2. Enter an email and password, or select **Continue with Google**. If your team sent you an invitation, open that link first.
3. Verify your email if you signed up with email and password. The console signs you in after verification.
4. Accept the required legal documents when the console asks you to do so.
5. The console selects an organization you already belong to. If you have none, it creates and selects a default workspace. Use the organization switcher to select or create another organization.

API keys, credits, and usage data belong to the selected organization. Check the organization switcher before you create a key or review usage.
