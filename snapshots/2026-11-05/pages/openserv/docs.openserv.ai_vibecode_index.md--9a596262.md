## Quickstart (Code)

Get going quickly:

1. Pick your SDK — [TypeScript](https://docs.openserv.ai/vibecode/sdk/typescript.md) or [Python](https://docs.openserv.ai/vibecode/sdk/python)
2. Or call the [REST API](https://docs.openserv.ai/api) directly
3. Build an agent — see [Build a custom agent](https://docs.openserv.ai/vibecode/build-custom-agent.md)
